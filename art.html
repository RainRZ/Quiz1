The Art Club provides students with a creative space where they can explore different forms of art and express their ideas. Members can learn new techniques, work on individual projects, and collaborate with other students.

The purpose of the Art Club is to encourage students to express their creativity, develop their artistic skills, and explore different forms of art in a fun and supportive environment.


Club Advisor : Terry Johnson

Painting Project:
Members create paintings using different techniques, styles, and materials.
Art Exhibition:
Students display their artwork for other students, teachers, and visitors to see.
Creative Workshop:
Members learn new artistic techniques through guided activities and hands-on projects.
