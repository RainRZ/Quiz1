Belize High School

Belize High School (BHS) is a private, coeducational secondary school located in Belize City, Belize, that features a college-preparatory and technology-integrated curriculum.

Established in September 2010 by the Board of Governors of the adjacent Belize Elementary School (BES), BHS spans four years of secondary education corresponding to standard high school levels.

Academics and CurriculumSpecialized Focus: It is known for integrating strong information technology (IT) and science tracks alongside traditional subjects.Course Offerings: The curriculum includes advanced math, sciences (biology, chemistry, physics), networking, programming, database management, and humanities.Requirements: Students must complete regular community service hours and meet credit thresholds across grade levels to graduate.
