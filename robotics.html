Robotics Club

Robotics is an interdisciplinary branch of engineering and computer science that deals with the design, construction, operation, and application of robots.

The primary goal of robotics is to create intelligent machines that can sense their environment, make decisions, and perform tasks autonomously or semi-autonomously to assist humans—especially in jobs that are repetitive, dirty, or dangerous.
