The Chess Club provides students with a fun environment to learn and play chess while improving their strategic thinking, concentration, and problem-solving skills.

The purpose of the Chess Club is to develop students' strategic thinking, concentration, problem-solving skills, and decision-making abilities through playing chess.


Club Advisor : Terry Johnson

Chess Tournament:
Members compete against each other in organized chess matches to test their skills and strategies.
Chess Lessons:
Students learn basic rules, opening strategies, tactics, and different ways to improve their gameplay.
Chess Challenge:
Members solve chess puzzles and strategic challenges to improve their critical thinking and decision-making.
